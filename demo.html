About & Speedrun Retiming Guide

Takes start and stop points of a YouTube video, down to the exact frame, and gives the time between them in the format 1h 23m 45s 678ms. Based on the original yt-frame-timer.

Step 1: Get Video Framerate

Right-click the YouTube player and select “Stats for nerds”. Look at the 3rd line (Current / Optimal Res) and enter the two digits after the @ symbol (e.g. 1920x1080@60 → 60 FPS).

Step 2: Copy Frame Debug Info

Pause the video and use the , and . keys to step to the exact start frame. Right-click the video → “Copy debug info” and paste it into Starting frame. Repeat for Ending frame.

Why YouTube Embeds Show “Error 153” & How This Tool Fixes It

YouTube's embedded player displays Error 153 (Video player configuration error) whenever a browser sandbox or local preview blocks the HTTP Referer header. This tool sets referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" on embeds and provides a built-in Storyboard & Frame Inspector plus a Local Video File Frame-Stepper that work in every browser environment without Error 153.
